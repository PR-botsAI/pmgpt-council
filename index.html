The protocol

Not another chat window.
A controlled decision process.

Every council session moves through three deliberate phases designed to surface assumptions, expose disagreement, and preserve human judgment.

01
ROUND ONE

Independent proposals

Your prompt is sent to multiple models in parallel. Each produces an independent answer before seeing any competing response.

  • Parallel generation
  • Provider diversity
  • Streaming side-by-side
02
ROUND TWO

Cross-examination

Models critique one another’s assumptions and risks. You can accept strong claims, deny weak ones, or simply listen.

  • Inter-model critique
  • Accept / Deny / Listen
  • Established truth context
03
FINAL ROUND

Blind consensus

Responses are anonymized, scored, and synthesized into a final recommendation with agreements, conflicts, and caveats intact.

  • Anonymous evaluation
  • Multi-criteria scoring
  • Chairman synthesis

PMGPT Council is built around active oversight—not passive acceptance. Your decisions directly shape the context models receive in the next round.

Try the controls →
ACCEPT

Lock strong claims

Add a validated idea to the established context so every model must account for it in the next round.

DENY

Prune weak reasoning

Remove a flawed assumption before it contaminates the rest of the debate or consumes more tokens.

LISTEN

Let the debate develop

Stay neutral and allow the models to cross-examine the claim before you make a decision.
